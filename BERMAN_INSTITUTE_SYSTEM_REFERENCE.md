# Berman Institute — System Reference

## 1. System Overview

Berman Institute is a digital medical education ecosystem designed to train and continuously engage healthcare professionals in longevity medicine, metabolic health, peptide medicine, GLP-1 therapies, biomarkers, aesthetics, and other emerging areas of medicine. Rather than functioning as a traditional online course platform, the system combines structured professional programs, short courses, assessments, credentials, faculty-led live education, a searchable clinical library, research content, and a professional network inside a personalized “My Campus” experience.

The long-term model is designed around a professional lifecycle: **Discover → Learn → Complete → Earn Credential → Join Network → Participate → Contribute → Research → Teach**. The public website establishes the institute and its authority, My Campus delivers education and ongoing engagement, the Faculty Portal supports instructors and contributors, the Research / Editorial Portal manages evidence-based content and submissions, and Admin manages users, roles, programs, payments, analytics, and integrations.

---

## 2. Core Product Principles

- The user should experience **Berman Institute**, not a generic LMS.
- The public website sells the **institution and programs**.
- My Campus delivers the **education and professional experience**.
- Research builds **authority and ongoing relevance**.
- Network creates **retention, community, and future contributors**.
- Faculty expands the institute beyond Dr. Dean Berman alone.
- Short courses should feed users into deeper programs.
- Advanced programs should lead into membership and the professional network.
- Research and community activity should eventually generate new educational content.

---

## 3. Grand Rounds

**Grand Rounds** are recurring educational sessions where physicians and faculty discuss important clinical topics, complex cases, new research, or emerging treatments. Traditionally, hospitals and medical schools use Grand Rounds as a formal continuing-education format for clinicians.

For Berman Institute, Grand Rounds should function as a **live monthly expert session** with Dr. Dean Berman and guest faculty. A typical session can include:

1. Research update
2. Clinical case presentation
3. Faculty discussion
4. Q&A
5. Recording added to the Clinical Library
6. Optional summary, quiz, research brief, and short clips

Example:

> **Berman Institute Grand Rounds**  
> *GLP-1 Beyond Weight Loss: Complex Clinical Cases*  
> Dr. Dean Berman + Guest Faculty  
> Live Case Review • Evidence Update • Q&A

Alternative user-facing labels:
- **Live Clinical Rounds**
- **Expert Clinical Sessions**
- **Faculty Case Conferences**

# 8. Recommended Technical Positioning

LearnWorlds should function as the **learning engine** for:
- Users
- Enrollments
- Courses
- Learning programs
- Progress
- Assessments
- Certificates
- Community
- Live learning
- Membership access

External systems should handle more specialized capabilities such as:
- CRM
- Professional license document storage / verification
- Formal research project management
- Clinical research databases
- Berman AI
- Advanced analytics
- External marketing systems

The learner should still experience a single coherent Berman Institute product regardless of how many services are connected behind the scenes.

# 10. North Star

The system should make a healthcare professional feel:

> “I joined a professional medical institute, not an online course website. I can study, earn credentials, learn from faculty, stay current with research, participate in a professional network, and continue developing within the institute after completing my program.”

The long-term institutional loop is:

**Education → Credentials → Network → Contributors → Faculty → Research → New Knowledge → New Education**
